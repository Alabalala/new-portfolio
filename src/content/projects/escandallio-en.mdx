That chefs tend to focus more on catering great food at restaurants instead of number is a known fact.

This app was developed exclusively for a client that wanted a simple, fast and reliable app to to gain total transparency over their menu profitability.

Escandallio centralises ingredient catalogs and automates complex calculations. Bye bye to paper and bye bye to myriad of excel sheets.

I designed this app to provide real-time financial metrics, including precise per-dish costs, profit margins, and VAT impacts.

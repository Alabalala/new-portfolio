A charming place needs a charming website.

Girasol B&B is a small eco-friendly bed and breakfast in the mountains of Spain. They needed a website that reflected their values and the beauty of their location. I designed and developed a responsive website that showcases their rooms, amenities, and the surrounding nature.

The website is built with Astro, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, and Sanity Studio for the client's easy content management.
